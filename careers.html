Guest Relations Executive

Customer Relations 1 - 3 Years Exp Full-Time

Job Objective:

Act as the primary customer experience ambassador at our central help desks, welcoming guests, managing enquiries, and resolving local complaints.

Requirements:

  • Excellent communication skills in Malayalam and English (Hindi/Arabic is a plus).
  • Friendly customer-first attitude with presentable aesthetics.
  • Proficient with basic spreadsheet management and call transfers.

Assistant Store Manager (Anchor Store)

Retail Sales 3 - 5 Years Exp Full-Time

Job Objective:

Assist in daily retail store operations, manage staff duties, track inventory sales levels, and coordinate customer support teams to maximize retail benchmarks.

Requirements:

  • Prior experience managing retail sales teams or apparel outlets.
  • Strong leadership, training, and schedule coordination capabilities.
  • Graduate degree in Business Administration, Marketing, or relevant field.

IT Systems & CCTV Support Engineer

IT Support 2 - 4 Years Exp Full-Time

Job Objective:

Maintain the mall's high-speed WiFi connections, digital interactive store locators, ticketing systems, and central CCTV server configurations.

Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree or Diploma in Computer Science/IT or Electronic Engineering.
  • Hands-on experience with surveillance networks and local area configurations.
  • Rapid problem-solving for network downtime.
