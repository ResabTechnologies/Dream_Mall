Local Heritage

Payyoli: The Land of Gold & Athletics

Payyoli is a historic, picturesque coastal town nestled on the banks of Moorad River in Kozhikode district, Kerala. Known globally as the home of the legendary athlete P.T. Usha (the "Payyoli Express"), this region holds deep historical and cultural value.

It features pristine natural attractions, such as the famous Payyoli Beach with its shallow waters, which is a major eco-tourism site for observing endangered Olive Ridley turtles. Nearby lies the historical Kunjali Marakkar museum, honoring the naval commanders of the Zamorin, who fought against colonial powers on these shores.

Dream Mall respects and honors this rich legacy. By setting up this modern commercial space, we bring new business energy, local employment opportunities, and tourism support, strengthening Payyoli's position on Kerala's modern map.
