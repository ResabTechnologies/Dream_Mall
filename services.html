Premium Guest Facilities

We pride ourselves on offering world-class services to make your shopping trip comfortable, safe, and absolute fun.

Valet & Basement Parking

Our spacious multi-level basement parking accommodates 500+ cars and 800+ two-wheelers. Safe, well-lit, with automated license plate ticketing.

24/7 ATM Services

Access cash quickly at our dedicated ATM lobby located on the ground floor next to the hypermarket, featuring leading nationalized banks.

Executive Prayer Rooms

Separate prayer spaces for men and women are available on the third floor. Fully carpeted, calm environment, equipped with wash/ablution stations.

Baby Care Rooms

Clean and peaceful nursing suites on all floors. Equipped with changing tables, warm water, bottle heaters, and comfortable feeding chairs.

Wheelchair Access

Dream Mall is fully barrier-free. Complimentary wheelchairs are available at help desks. All entries have ramps and wide-access elevator doors.

24/7 Security & CCTV

Enjoy shopping under the secure protection of continuous CCTV surveillance, metal detection gates, and professional security patrols.

Central Information Desk

Located in the Ground Floor main lobby. Our team assists with store directions, lost and found logs, local transport coordination, and paging announcements.

High-Speed Free WiFi

Stay connected online. Register your phone number to access high-speed unlimited WiFi throughout all shopping lanes and eating lounges.

Emergency First Aid Room

Fully equipped first aid room with certified medical responders on standby during operating hours. Directly connected with ambulance services.
