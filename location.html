Transportation & Access

Dream Mall is located directly adjacent to National Highway 66 (NH 66) bypass stretch, making it extremely accessible from any direction in Kozhikode district.

By Train

Payyoli Railway Station (PYOL) is just 1.5 km away. Autos and cabs are available 24/7 at the station exit to reach the mall directly.

By Bus

Any KSRTC or private express bus heading on the Kozhikode-Kannur route stops at Payyoli Central Bus Stand. The mall is a 3-minute walk from the stand.

By Air

Calicut International Airport (CCJ) is located 65 km South. Kannur International Airport (CNN) is located 72 km North. Pre-paid airport taxi bookings are supported.

Nearby Tourist Attractions

Combine your luxury shopping experience with a visit to Payyoli Beach and the Sand Banks (2.5 km West), or visit the historical Kunjali Marakkar museum at Iringal (3 km North).
